Mapcoder

Before you write

A few quick checks for common issues:

  • Mapcoder isn't in the share sheet? Look in both places: the icon row at the top, and the actions list below it, where it reads Get map code. If it's missing from that list, scroll to the bottom of the share sheet, tap Edit Actions, and add Get map code. It stays there from then on.
  • Nothing happens when you share from another map app? Other map apps share a short link that holds no coordinates, so Mapcoder does not appear in their share sheet. Share from Apple Maps.
  • The My location button doesn't work? Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mapcoder and choose While Using the App.
  • The My location button gives a less precise code than expected? Turn on Precise Location: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mapcoder → Precise Location. Without it the app can only place you in the block or zone you're in — you can still tap down to your exact spot on the map.
  • The Discover map stays blank? Its background map is loaded from the internet, so it needs a connection. Getting map codes doesn't.
  • Your car navigation system doesn't accept a code, or lands somewhere else? Email us the place, the code the app gave, and your car navigation model, and we'll look into it.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is a map code?
    A string of numbers that stands for a spot in Japan. Many Japanese car navigation systems let you enter one as a destination, which is quicker than typing an address or phone number, and works for places that have neither.

  • Does it need an internet connection?
    No. Codes are worked out on your iPhone without any network request. Only the background map in the Discover tab is loaded from the internet.

  • Is this an official Denso app?
    No. Mapcoder is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed from Denso. Always confirm the place on your car's screen before you drive.

  • Where is my history kept?
    Only on your iPhone. It is never uploaded. Clearing the History tab deletes it, and deleting the app removes it entirely.

  • Can the developer see where I look things up?
    No. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics. Your places never leave your phone.

Manual

Getting a map code

Find the place in Apple Maps, tap Share, and choose Mapcoder — either the icon in the top row, or Get map code in the list below it. The coordinates travel with the share itself, so the code appears straight away, with no network request. Type it into your car navigation system as the destination, then check the place on the car's screen before you set off.

Where you are now

The My location button turns your current position into a map code, which is handy for saving where you parked or telling someone where to meet. It needs permission to use your location while the app is open. Your position is used on your iPhone and never sent anywhere.

History

Every place you look up is kept in the History tab, so you can find a code again without going back to Apple Maps. Places you get through the share sheet land there too. Clear the History tab to delete them.

Discover

The Discover tab shows a zoomable map of Japan with the map code zones drawn over it. The background map is 地理院タイル from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan, loaded live while you look at it.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Mapcoder collects nothing.

There is no analytics, no tracking and no account. When you share a place from Apple Maps, the coordinates travel with the share itself, and the map code is worked out on this phone with no network request.

The Discover tab is the one exception: it draws its background from an online map service, which means that service sees the map area you are looking at. It is never told anything about your locations or your history. Leave that tab alone and the app makes no network requests at all.

Your history is stored only on this device, in a container shared between the app and its two extensions. It is never uploaded. Clearing the History tab deletes it, and deleting the app removes it entirely.

Location Permission

Mapcoder asks for access to your location (while using the app) only for the optional "My location" button, which turns where you are into a map code on your iPhone. Your position is never sent anywhere.
