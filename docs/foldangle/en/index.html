Fold Angle

Your folding iPhone already knows how far it's open. Fold Angle turns the hinge into a protractor: set the phone into a corner, against a door, or over the edge of a box, and read the angle between the two halves. On other iPhones and iPads it works as a tilt meter.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Which devices does it work on?
    Fold Angle is built for iPhone Duo, where it reads the hinge angle directly. On other iPhones and iPads it works as a tilt meter: it shows how far the device leans from level, using the motion sensors.

  • How do I keep a reading?
    Tap Hold to freeze the number, then lift the phone away and read it. Tap Release to go back to live readings. You can also double-tap the number.

  • What does Set Zero do?
    It makes the current angle the new zero, so you can compare two corners or measure how far a door swings. Tap Clear Zero to go back to absolute angles.

  • What are Inside and Outside?
    Inside is the angle between the two screens. Outside is the angle on the back of the fold (360° minus the inside). Tap the button to switch.

  • Lying flat, it shows about 2° instead of 0°.
    In tilt mode the camera bump lifts one end of the phone. Tap Set Zero on the flat surface first and measure from there, or lay the phone on its screen.

  • I don't feel the taps.
    Fold Angle taps lightly every 15° and firmly at exactly 90°. If you feel nothing, check that Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics is on.

  • How accurate is it?
    It's made for quick everyday checks, not calibrated precision work. For anything that has to be exact, confirm with a real protractor or level.

  • Does it collect my data?
    No. Fold Angle has no account, no servers and makes no network requests. Sensor readings stay on your device.
