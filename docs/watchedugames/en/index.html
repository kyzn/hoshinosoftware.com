Doremi Teacher Watch Special

Ear training on your wrist. A note plays, you pick its name from four.

  • Choose Do Re Mi or A B C names.
  • Practice the seven natural notes, or add sharps and flats.
  • My instrument: tell the app which note your Re sounds like, and every name follows. Made for saz players, useful for any transposing instrument.
  • Stats for today and all time, overall and per note set.

Morse Teacher Watch Special

Learn International Morse code for A to Z, in both directions.

  • Morse → Letter: the watch plays a letter, you choose it from four.
  • Letter → Morse: see a letter, tap it out with the Dot and Dash keys, then Check. Each tap sounds its own tone.
  • Character speed from 8 to 25 words per minute. Start slow and work up to what you hear on the air.
  • Stats for today and all time.

In both apps

  • Replay the sound as often as you like before answering.
  • Every round ends with the right answer, what you chose, and a button to hear it again.
  • No timers, no penalty for replaying.
  • The Digital Crown adjusts the volume while you practice. Bluetooth headphones work too.
  • Standalone on Apple Watch. No iPhone app, no account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.
