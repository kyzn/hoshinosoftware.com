Before you write

A few quick checks for common issues:

  • Camera not working? Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → enable access for Membership Navi.
  • Cards not appearing on another device? Make sure both devices are signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled. The Settings tab in the app shows whether iCloud Sync is on. First sync can take a minute.
  • Barcode not reading at the register? The screen goes to full brightness automatically while a code is displayed (you can toggle this in Settings). If a specific shop's scanner still refuses a code, email us the shop and card type and we'll investigate. Faithful reproduction is this app's whole job.
  • Watch shows "Could not generate code"? Open that card once on your iPhone and give iCloud a moment to sync, then try again on the watch.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Will the barcode really scan like my physical card?
    Yes. Membership Navi remembers the exact barcode format and settings at scan time (Code 128, EAN, Codabar, QR, and so on) and regenerates the code in the same configuration, not a generic re-encoding.

  • What happens if I lose my phone or reinstall the app?
    Your cards live in your personal iCloud. Sign into the same Apple Account and they come back automatically.

  • Does it work on Apple Watch?
    Yes. The watch app shows your cards full-screen on a white background for easy scanning, and it keeps its own copy of your cards, so it works even when your iPhone isn't with you.

  • Does it need an internet connection?
    Only for iCloud sync. Showing your cards and their codes works completely offline.

  • Can the developer see my cards?
    No. Your data is stored on your devices and in your personal iCloud database, which only you can access. There is no server of ours, no account, and no analytics.

  • Which code types are supported?
    QR, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, ITF, Codabar, PDF417, Aztec, and plain member numbers.

  • Why can't I edit a scanned code's number?
    By design. A scanned code is reproduced exactly as it was on your card, so editing it would break that guarantee. To change a card's code, rescan the card. Cards you typed in by hand stay editable.

  • Does it access my entire photo library?
    No. You pick a single photo with the system photo picker; the app never receives access to your library.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Introduction

Membership Navi is built around a simple principle: your data belongs to you. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Camera Access

The app uses your device's camera to scan barcodes and QR codes on your cards. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device. No images or video frames are ever stored, uploaded, or shared with anyone.

Photo Access

When you add a card from a photo, you pick a single image using the system photo picker. The app never receives access to your photo library.

Your Cards and iCloud

The cards you save are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your personal iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so they appear on all your devices, including Apple Watch, and are restored when you reinstall the app. This database belongs to your Apple Account: we cannot read, access, or share its contents. We operate no servers of our own.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. The app does not require an account or any form of registration, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Purchases

Membership Navi is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive your Apple ID, payment method, or any other payment information.

Children's Privacy

Because no personal data is collected by us, Membership Navi complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.
