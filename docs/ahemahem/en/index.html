Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I set it up?
    Open the app and go through the four steps: allow Screen Time access, allow notifications, pick the apps to nag about, and turn nagging on.

  • When do the nags arrive?
    After a few minutes of use, then on and off for as long as you keep going. They come irregularly on purpose, so you can't tune them out, and they get less frequent over time.

  • Is the time per app or in total?
    In total. Time spent in all the apps you picked adds up, and the count starts again at midnight.

  • Why do nags say "the doom app" instead of the real app name?
    iOS keeps your choice of apps private, even from Ahem Ahem. The app only ever gets anonymous tokens, so it makes up a name instead: the doom app, the scroll machine, the pocket casino and many more.

  • I'm not getting any nags.
    Check that all four setup steps show as on, and that a Focus mode such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them. Time only counts while one of the picked apps is open on screen. iOS decides exactly when to report usage, so a nag can occasionally arrive late or be skipped.

  • Does it block apps?
    No. Ahem Ahem only nags. It never locks, hides, or limits any app.

  • How do I make it stop?
    Turn off Nagging in the app. You can also remove its access in Settings → Screen Time, or delete the app.

  • Does it collect my data?
    No. Ahem Ahem makes no network requests, has no account, and stores nothing outside your iPhone.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Introduction

Ahem Ahem is built around a simple principle: your data belongs to you. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled. In short, nothing leaves your device.

Screen Time Data

With your permission, Ahem Ahem uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls and Device Activity) to learn when you have spent a certain amount of time in the apps you picked. iOS gives the app only anonymous tokens for those apps, never their names, and never shows it what you do inside them. This information stays on your iPhone and is used only to time the nags.

Notifications

Nags are local notifications created on your iPhone. No notification content is sent to or from any server.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, track, or process any personal information. The app does not require an account or any form of registration.

No Internet Connection

Ahem Ahem makes no network requests. Your data never leaves your device.

Purchases

Any purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your Apple ID, payment method, or any other payment information.

Third-Party Services

Ahem Ahem does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any third party.

Children's Privacy

Because no personal data is collected, Ahem Ahem complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.
